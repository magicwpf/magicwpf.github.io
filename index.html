Pengfei Wan

I am the founder and head of the Kling models and data team (aka the Kling Team) at Kuaishou Technology. I used to be the head of the AI department (MT Lab) at Meitu, Inc. I did my Ph.D. at the ECE Dept. of HKUST, and B.E. at the EEIS Dept. of USTC.

Kling Team is building next-generation multimodal world models across video, audio, text, 3D, and beyond. Our work spans, but is not limited to:

  • Multimodal Understanding & Reasoning
  • Multimodal Generation & Interaction
  • Multimodal Data Systems & Algorithms

We are continuously seeking outstanding talents to join us. Feel free to reach out!
